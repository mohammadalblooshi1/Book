# *Never Say Your Name to the Wind*: Part I as Anime Comic Pages
### Prompts for Gemini (gemini.google.com)

**How to use**
1. Open Gemini and choose image generation. Ask: *"Create an image."*
2. Paste **one whole page prompt** below. Each prompt already contains the style, the characters and the panels, so they work on their own.
3. If a character looks different from earlier pages, upload your best earlier page and add: *"Keep the characters looking exactly like this reference."*
4. To fix one thing, reply to the image in Gemini: *"Same page, but make Ghareeb's left eye silver."*
5. Gemini sometimes misspells speech-bubble text. If it does, ask *"Fix the text in the bubbles"* or add the words yourself later.

There are **30 pages**: 2 per chapter for Chapters 1–15.

---

## STYLE (already inside every prompt)
> Full-colour vertical comic page (manga/manhwa layout, 2:3 portrait), semi-realistic Japanese anime / seinen manga art, detailed and cinematic, not chibi, not cartoonish. Dramatic lighting, desert golds and dusk oranges against cold ash-greys, clean black panel borders, English speech bubbles and caption boxes. 1920s Arabian desert (UAE and Oman): mud-brick houses, palm-frond huts, ghaf trees, dunes, Bedouin clothing (kandura, ghutra, abaya, goat-hair cloaks). Horror mood: dread, shadows, eerie silence.

## CHARACTERS (already inside every prompt that needs them)
- **THEYAB**: 15-year-old Arab boy, thin, sun-darkened skin, sun-bleached messy hair, very light grey-gold eyes, ragged sand-coloured kandura, always keeps his **left fist closed**; a faint **glowing golden feather-shaped mark** on his chest; when using his power, **golden cracks** glow on his arms and face.
- **GHAREEB**: an old **saluki** dog, lean and sand-coloured, feathered ears and tail, **one amber eye and one glowing silver eye**; his shadow is slightly too long.
- **UMM HANAN**: old widow, black abaya and face-veil (batoola), kind tired eyes, flour on her hands, carries an oil lamp.
- **NADIM**: 15-year-old bully, broad, sharp face, white kandura. **SHIBL**: his little brother, 7, big eyes, a blue bead on a string.
- **SAQI**: the well-keeper, their father, heavy, grey beard.
- **OLD BAKHIT**: very old blind healer, milky eyes, long white beard, tent full of hanging herbs and small brass bells.
- **MUTAWWA' HILAL**: about 60, thin, sun-blackened, white beard, goat-hair cloak, walking stick, leather satchel.
- **GHANIM**: fat trader, hennaed beard, crooked brass scales.
- **HADDAJ AL-HAFAR**: old Bedouin sorcerer, clay under his fingernails, hooded, carrying a battered book.
- **THE OLD MAN AT THE FIRE**: very old Bedouin man (face mostly in shadow), stammering, pouring coffee from a dallah, with children around a winter fire; an ancient saluki with one scarred-shut eyelid at his feet.

## CHAPTER 1: NEVER WALK AT NOON

### Page 1
> [STYLE] [THE OLD MAN AT THE FIRE, GHAREEB]
> Panel 1 (wide): Night winter camp in the Liwa dunes, a fire, eleven Bedouin children sitting close; an old man with face in shadow pours coffee from a brass dallah with shaking hands. Caption: "The teller said… Hold your breath, little ones."
> Panel 2: Close-up of the old man's lips. Speech: "You'll say it's a tale. I tell you it happened. I was there."
> Panel 3: Close-up of an ancient saluki at his feet: one amber eye, the other eyelid scarred shut, and something faintly glowing under the scar. A child points.
> Panel 4 (big, bottom half): Flash-forward: a boy standing on top of a gigantic dune under a huge full moon, arms open, golden fire bursting from his chest into enormous wings of light. Caption: "But that is the end."

### Page 2
> [STYLE] [THEYAB as a 7-year-old child, GHAREEB]
> Panel 1: A dreamlike hidden world underground: giant tree roots like pillars, wells of glowing sweet water, soft light with no sun; a small boy playing beside an old saluki. Caption: "Before the beginning, there was the Quiet Country."
> Panel 2: A gazelle with glowing gold eyes, half woman in silhouette, watching the boy lovingly; a faint smell-like mist of white.
> Panel 3: The boy following a gazelle up a narrow shimmering passage of light. Caption: "He followed it too far."
> Panel 4: Empty white-hot noon lane of a mud-brick village. The small boy lies alone in the sand, crying. An old saluki sits watching him. Distant women's voices in small bubbles: "Come in from the sun!" "The Noon Mare will take you!" Caption: "No one called him."
> Panel 5: Eight years later: teenage Theyab digging alone in half-buried ruins at noon, left fist closed. On a broken wall, a gazelle with gold eyes watches.
> Panel 6 (hook): Night. Round hoofprints, two by two, circle a sleeping Theyab's blanket and stop. Ghareeb's silver eye glows.
