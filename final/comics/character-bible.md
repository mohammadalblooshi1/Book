# Character Bible for the Comic (visual reference)

Use these descriptions **word for word** in every slide where the character appears, so they look the same on every page.

## Global art rules
- Semi-realistic **anime / seinen manga** style, cinematic, full colour, horror mood. Not chibi, not cartoon.
- 1920s **UAE and Oman Bedouin** world: mud-brick houses, palm-frond huts (*barasti*), ghaf trees, dunes, falaj channels, brass coffee pots (*dallah*), camels, salukis.
- **All people are Arab, with brown skin and Arab features.**
- **Men and boys:** short hair, **bare heads (no ghutra, no agal, no turban)**, kandura or bisht.
- **Women:** fully and modestly covered, with long abaya, long sleeves, and **hair fully covered** by a shayla. Older women may wear the gold *burqa* face mask.
- **Everyone is always fully clothed.** Nothing revealing, ever.
- **The ones from below** are drawn as creatures, never as attractive people.

## People
| Name | Look (copy exactly) |
|---|---|
| **Theyab** | Male, 15, Emirati Bedouin boy. Thin, brown sun-darkened skin, thick black eyebrows, **short black hair**, light amber-gold eyes. Ragged sand-coloured kandura. A faint glowing **golden feather mark** on his chest. Keeps his **left fist closed**. When he uses the Light, **golden cracks** glow on his arms and face |
| **Ghareeb** | An old **Arabian saluki hound** (a real dog breed, not a fox): very slim and long-legged, deep narrow chest, long narrow head, **long silky feathered ears**, feathered curved tail, sand-coloured coat. **Left eye glowing silver, right eye amber.** His shadow is a little too long |
| **Umm Hanan** | Old woman, black abaya and black shayla covering all her hair, **gold burqa face mask**, kind tired eyes, wrinkled brown hands, flour on her fingers, often holding a small oil lamp that burns steady |
| **Nadim** | Male, 15, broad-shouldered, short black hair, hard proud face, white kandura |
| **Shibl** | Male, 7, small, short black hair, huge dark eyes, a **blue bead** on a string at his neck |
| **Saqi** | Male, about 45, heavy, short grey hair, grey beard, rough hands, well-keeper's rope over his shoulder |
| **Ghanim** | Male, about 50, fat, short hair, **orange henna-dyed beard**, gold rings, crooked **brass scales** |
| **Old Bakhit** | Male, ancient, blind with **milky white eyes**, short white hair, long white beard, plain robe; a tent full of hanging herbs and **small brass bells** |
| **Mutawwa' Hilal** | Male, about 60, thin, very dark sun-blackened skin, short white hair, neat white beard, **brown goat-hair cloak**, wooden staff, leather satchel, a worn old book |
| **Haddaj the Digger** | Male, old and gaunt, hawk nose, short grey hair, dark bisht, **clay under his fingernails**, a battered book of buried places, a short shovel |
| **The Nameless** | Riders in grey robes with **smooth white faceless masks**, on **pale camels**, carrying grey blades |
| **The old man at the fire** | Male, very old, short white hair, white beard, brown bisht, pouring coffee from a brass dallah with shaking hands, face lit by a winter fire. At his feet lies an ancient saluki whose **left eyelid is scarred shut** |

## The ones from below (Part I)
| Name | Look |
|---|---|
| **The Noon Mare** | A creature in white noon heat-haze: from the waist up a pale figure in a long, faded, modest dress, with **hair and face hidden under a dark head-cloth**; from the waist down **a grey donkey's legs and hooves**. She is never shown clearly, always half-hidden in the shimmer |
| **The Grey** | A flat **ash-coloured fog** that drains all colour from whatever it touches. Inside it there is no sound |
| **The emptied** | People with **ash-grey skin and blank white eyes**, standing still, repeating their last words |
| **The Worn** (possessed) | A normal person whose **eyes roll completely white**, with black smoke at the mouth, a doubled voice, and a twisted, unnatural pose |
| **The Twin** | (Voice only in Part I.) Shown as Theyab's own speech bubble with no speaker |
| **The Lamp-Keeper** (good) | A small hooded figure made of **warm amber light**, cupping a lamp flame with both hands |
| **The Sand-Guide** (good) | Usually a **gazelle with glowing gold eyes**. In the Quiet Country, a tall gentle figure in long white robes with a **white veil covering her hair**, gold eyes, her feet not touching the sand |
| **The Quiet Country** | The good part of the Underneath: giant ghaf roots like pillars, wells of glowing sweet water, soft light with no sun |
