# Chapter 9: Always Let the Mutawwa' In

## Slide 1 — Peace on This House
**Scene:** Early morning, the gate of Sheikh Mazyad's yard. Wide establishing shot from inside the yard: the thin old stranger leans on his staff in the gateway, looking over everything in turn — the matchlock men standing up too fast, Mazyad still pulling his robe straight, Saqi tied to the post, Theyab against the far wall, and, longest of all, Ghareeb. A single cracked laugh loosens the yard.
**Characters:**
- Mutawwa' Hilal — Male, about 60, thin, very dark sun-blackened skin, short white hair, neat white beard, brown goat-hair cloak, wooden staff, leather satchel, a worn old book.
- Sheikh Mazyad — Male, about 60, short grey hair, bare head, beard dyed dark red with henna, fine brown bisht over a white kandura.
- Saqi (Worn) — Male, about 45, heavy, short grey hair, grey beard, rough hands, well-keeper's rope over his shoulder; eyes rolled completely white, black smoke at the mouth, twisted unnatural pose (tied to the post, broken arm bound across his chest, no smile).
- Theyab — Male, 15, Emirati Bedouin boy. Thin, brown sun-darkened skin, thick black eyebrows, short black hair, light amber-gold eyes. Ragged sand-coloured kandura. A faint glowing golden feather mark on his chest. Keeps his left fist closed. (Faint gold cracks on his forearms.)
- Ghareeb — An old Arabian saluki hound (a real dog breed, not a fox): very slim and long-legged, deep narrow chest, long narrow head, long silky feathered ears, feathered curved tail, sand-coloured coat. Left eye glowing silver, right eye amber. His shadow is a little too long.
**Text:**
- Hilal: Peace on this house.
- Mazyad: You are welcome, uncle. Our house is your house. Three days.
- Hilal: Then let's start with coffee. He'll keep. You've tied him to a post. Posts are underrated.

**Text:**

## Slide 5 — "Monster" and the Line in His Hand
**Scene:** The white eyes find Theyab across the yard. In the dead mother's soft voice the thing names him "monster" for the first time, and the word settles into the crowd like a seed. Then it mentions the line in his hand — and Hilal stops reading for one breath; his eyes open on Theyab's fist, and on Ghareeb. Then he begins again, louder; the cool-water ripples surge like an opened sluice; breath smokes in the cold; Saqi goes rigid, rope taut, post creaking.
**Characters:**

- Mutawwa' Hilal — as Slide 1, eyes open.
- Theyab — as Slide 1.
- Ghareeb — as Slide 1.
**Text:**

- Saqi: *And ask him about the line in his hand. We already know what's in it. We've known since—*
- Caption (Ghareeb): It was the first time anyone had said it.
**Mood:** Accusing, charged, surging.

## Slide 6 — The Hiss
**Scene:** Climax of the casting out. Saqi's mouth hangs open; from deep inside comes a long hiss like a well poured on coals. Above his mouth the air shimmers — a colourless ripple of heat rising into the sun. People at the gate cover their faces. Three lanes away at the well, a sound like a heavy lid dropped on a jar. Final beat: rope slack, Saqi slumped; Hilal sweating, breathing; the water in the clay bowl gone dark ash-grey.
