# Chapter 2: Never Sell What the Sand Gave You

## Slide 1 — The Words Slip Away
**Scene:** Grey dawn light under the tent flap; doves start up in the palms outside. Theyab sits against the tent pole whispering the dream-words, losing them one by one, then opens his left fist and stares at the silver thaler. The saluki lies with his back flat, watching. Medium close-up, cool morning tones.
**Characters:** Theyab — male, 15, Emirati Bedouin boy, thin, brown sun-darkened skin, thick black eyebrows, short black hair, light amber-gold eyes, ragged sand-coloured kandura, a faint glowing golden feather mark on his chest, keeps his left fist closed. Ghareeb — an old Arabian saluki hound (a real dog breed, not a fox): very slim and long-legged, deep narrow chest, long narrow head, long silky feathered ears, feathered curved tail, sand-coloured coat, left eye glowing silver, right eye amber, his shadow a little too long.
**Text:**
- Theyab: "It was nothing. A dream-word. Everyone says dream-words."
- Caption: "Then he looked at the thaler, and the words were gone for good."
- Caption: "That's what silver is for, little one. Making you forget the thing you should be remembering."
**Mood:** Hazy, uneasy, quiet, tempting.

## Slide 9 — A Name Given to the Wind

Theyab sits hugging his knees and whispers his secret name; the wind lifts it away and returns cold, smelling of nothing, saying it back in his own emptied voice. Far south a flat grey band lies on the horizon and, above it, stars go out one by one. Wide shot from behind the boy, the grey line across the frame.
**Characters:** Theyab — male, 15, Emirati Bedouin boy, thin, brown sun-darkened skin, thick black eyebrows, short black hair, light amber-gold eyes, ragged sand-coloured kandura, a faint golden feather mark burning on his chest, left fist closed. **Text:**
- Theyab: "Theyab."
