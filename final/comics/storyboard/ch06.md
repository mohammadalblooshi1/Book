# Chapter 6: Never Answer When the Grey Calls

## Slide 1 — The Village Shut Like a Fist
**Scene:** Just after the last light. Wide high-angle shot over the dark mud-brick village of Hilat al-Sarab: every lamp out, every door barred. To the east, the Grey lies flat over the lanes it has drained. At the dry well, two sets of footprints face the bucket, with no prints walking away. In the foreground, Theyab's tent sits alone in its hollow past the last house, Theyab in its mouth with his khanjar across his knees, Ghareeb lying across his feet.
**Characters:**
- Theyab — Male, 15, Emirati Bedouin boy. Thin, brown sun-darkened skin, thick black eyebrows, short black hair, light amber-gold eyes. Ragged sand-coloured kandura. A faint glowing golden feather mark on his chest. Keeps his left fist closed.
- Ghareeb — An old Arabian saluki hound (a real dog breed, not a fox): very slim and long-legged, deep narrow chest, long narrow head, long silky feathered ears, feathered curved tail, sand-coloured coat. Left eye glowing silver, right eye amber. His shadow is a little too long.
**Text:**
- Caption (Ghareeb): Listen to me, little one. A voice is the last thing to leave.
- Caption (Ghareeb): The night after the first emptied stood smiling at the well, the Grey learned to talk.
**Mood:** Hushed, barred, waiting dread.

## Slide 2 — Voices at the Doors
**Scene:** Night, low angle down a barred lane at the edge of the Grey. Faint pale wisps drift out of the fog toward the closed wooden doors, each wisp suggesting a voice (a small child's handprint shape near a door, an old man's cough). Behind one door, a woman's hand is pressed over her own mouth in the dark. Inset close-up: Theyab's cold hand on Ghareeb's neck, the dog's hackles lying flat.
**Characters:**
- Theyab — as Slide 1.
- Ghareeb — as Slide 1.
- A village mother behind a door — woman fully covered in black abaya and black shayla over all her hair, hand over her mouth.
**Text:**
- Bubble (from the Grey, no speaker, wavering lettering): Yumma? Yumma, I'm cold. Let me in.
- Bubble (from the Grey): Who's barred the door on me? Open it, I'm tired.
- Theyab: Those are dead people.
- Caption (Ghareeb): It is called the Thousand-Voiced. It does not want to talk to you. It wants you to open the door.
**Mood:** Creeping, intimate, wrong.

## Slide 3 — The Lamp That Does Not Flicker

Other doorways along the lane show lamps guttered blue at the root. Theyab walks down through the palms toward the light, khanjar in hand, Ghareeb at his side with ears flat to his skull. A faint warm presence is felt at the threshold, never shown.
**Characters:**
- Theyab — as Slide 1, khanjar in hand.
- Ghareeb — as Slide 1, ears flattened.
**Text:**
- Caption (Ghareeb): Her lamp was the only light in Hilat al-Sarab. Hers did not even flicker.
- Caption (Ghareeb): Something from under the ground had once been treated kindly there, and had never forgotten.
**Mood:** Fragile warmth against dark.

## Slide 4 — Her Sons Come Home Wet
**Scene:** Night, over-the-shoulder from behind Umm Hanan in her open doorway, the steady lamp behind her making her a black shape with a yellow edge. Up the lane, where the colour stops, the dark mouth of the Grey glistens as if wet; drips of seawater fall on dry sand from nowhere. She steps off her threshold, face calm. Theyab drops his khanjar and lunges.
**Characters:**
- Umm Hanan — Old woman, black abaya and black shayla covering all her hair, gold burqa face mask, kind tired eyes, wrinkled brown hands, flour on her fingers, often holding a small oil lamp that burns steady (here the lamp stands in the niche behind her).
- Theyab — as Slide 1.
- Ghareeb — as Slide 1.
**Text:**
- Bubble (from the Grey, wet dripping lettering): Yumma. The boat's in. Open the door.
- Bubble (from the Grey): Yumma, I'm so cold. I've been so cold.
- Umm Hanan: I'm here, my eyes. I'm coming.
**Mood:** Grief pulled toward horror.

## Slide 5 — The Slap and the Hand
**Scene:** Night, tight two-shot in the lamplight. Theyab holds Umm Hanan round the waist from behind, heels dug into the sand; she twists and strikes his face with her open hand, his head snapping sideways. Next beat in the same slide (split panel): she lays the same hand flat on his cheek over the red four-finger mark, and behind her, inside the door, the lamp flame stands up tall, white and still, throwing both their shadows to the edge of the Grey.
**Characters:**
- Umm Hanan — as Slide 4.
- Theyab — as Slide 1, a red handprint rising on his cheek.
**Text:**
- Umm Hanan: Laqeet. Let me go, it's my son.
- Theyab: It isn't. It isn't, Umm Hanan, it isn't.
- Bubble (from the Grey, drowned voice): Laqeet. Let my mother go.
- Caption (Ghareeb): Nobody had ever touched his face like that before. He did not flinch.
**Mood:** Raw, tender, blazing.

## Slide 6 — The Goat-Herd Steps In
**Scene:** Starlight on the southern edge, wide shot. The goat-herd, sleepwalking, stands with one foot in starlight and one on the flat dead sand at the edge of the Grey. Just inside the fog, a woman-shaped fold in the darkness, like creased cloth, tilts its "head". Ghareeb sprints low and long toward him, ten strides off; Theyab shouts behind, his shout visibly dying in the air. The goat-herd steps in.
**Characters:**
- Theyab — as Slide 1.
- Ghareeb — as Slide 1, running flat out.
- The goat-herd — quiet Bedouin man, about 35, short black hair, bare head, thin beard, a split upper lip from a camel's kick, worn brown kandura, hands held out before him.
- The fold in the dark — a woman-shape made only of folded darkness, no face, no body, never clear.
**Text:**
- Bubble (from the fold): Are you going to leave me standing out here?
- Goat-herd: Is that you? Is it really you?
- Goat-herd: I'm here.
- Caption (Ghareeb): I was ten strides off.
**Mood:** Helpless, tragic, eerie.

## Slide 7 — He Came Back Smiling

The goat-herd walks out of the Grey exactly where he went in: ash-coloured skin, eyes white as the inside of a shell, his split lip turned up too far at both corners. He walks past Theyab and Ghareeb without seeing them and stands at his own hut door, palm flat on the wood. Theyab bites his own fist.

- Theyab — as Slide 1, fist pressed to his mouth.

## Slide 8 — Ghareeb Walks Into the Grey

**Characters:**
- Ghareeb — as Slide 1, silver eye blazing.
**Text:**
- Caption (Ghareeb): It goes where it's answered. Then let it listen to something else.
- Bubble (many voices, no speaker): Ah. You. You've been away. Come in. Come further in.
**Mood:** Brave, vast, uncanny.

## Slide 9 — Fifty Faces on the Roofs
**Scene:** Night, low angle from the lane up to the rooftops. Ghareeb sits in the middle of the lane, shaking, scratching behind his ear; Theyab stands beside him with his hand on his neck, Umm Hanan's handprint still on his cheek. Along the flat roofs, fifty villagers stand black against the stars, staring down. Ghanim points from his roof; Sheikh Mazyad watches from another.
**Characters:**
- Ghareeb — as Slide 1.
- Theyab — as Slide 1, red handprint on his cheek.
- Ghanim — Male, about 50, fat, short hair, orange henna-dyed beard, gold rings, crooked brass scales (hanging at his belt).
- Sheikh Mazyad — Male, about 60, short grey hair, bare head, beard dyed dark red with henna, fine brown bisht over a white kandura.
- Villagers — men with short hair and bare heads in kanduras; women fully covered in black abayas and shaylas over all their hair.
**Text:**
- Ghanim: It went in and it came out. They know it. It's one of them.
- Mazyad: Be quiet.
- Caption (Ghareeb): I watched fifty people who had just been saved decide they would rather not have been saved by me.
- Small whisper captions: The dog went in. / The dog brought it. / The dog came with the boy.
**Mood:** Accusing, cold, isolating.

## Slide 10 — The Well Hums Back
**Scene:** The next night, a little before midnight, from Ghareeb's hiding place in the shadow of the wall. Saqi, sick-faced and sleepless, lowers the bucket into the old well without naming the Maker, humming. The rope runs into the dark. Close-up down the stone shaft: far below, the black water shivers in rings as something sings the three notes back up. Inset: the row of eleven emptied in the dawn lanes before (a man with cupped hands at a palm, a girl with grey henna, a young mother rocking nothing).
**Characters:**
- Saqi — Male, about 45, heavy, short grey hair, grey beard, rough hands, well-keeper's rope over his shoulder (now running through his hands).
- Ghareeb — as Slide 1, half hidden at the wall.
- The emptied (inset) — villagers with ash-grey skin and blank white eyes, standing still; women among them fully covered with hair covered.
**Text:**
- Caption (Ghareeb): Eleven. In one night.
- Caption (Ghareeb): He did not name the Maker. He began to hum.
- Caption (Ghareeb): And from the bottom of the well, something hummed the song back to him.
- Musical notes rising from the shaft (no words).
**Mood:** Ominous, intimate, chilling.
