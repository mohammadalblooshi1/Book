# Chapter 3: Don't Let the Old Women Frighten the Children

## Slide 1 — Behind the Frond Wall
**Scene:** Noon. The grey still smears the south-eastern horizon, bleached by sun. In the white back lane Theyab sits in a slip of shade against the old grey palm-frond wall of Umm Hanan's summer room (arish), listening; the saluki rests its chin on his foot. Through the gaps in the fronds we glimpse the old woman and children lying on a mat. Wide shot along the lane, the grey haze in the far sky.
**Characters:** Theyab — male, 15, Emirati Bedouin boy, thin, brown sun-darkened skin, thick black eyebrows, short black hair, light amber-gold eyes, ragged sand-coloured kandura, a faint glowing golden feather mark on his chest, keeps his left fist closed. Ghareeb — an old Arabian saluki hound (a real dog breed, not a fox): very slim and long-legged, deep narrow chest, long narrow head, long silky feathered ears, feathered curved tail, sand-coloured coat, left eye glowing silver, right eye amber, his shadow a little too long. Umm Hanan (glimpsed inside) — old woman, black abaya and black shayla covering all her hair, gold burqa face mask, kind tired eyes, wrinkled brown hands, flour on her fingers. Shibl (glimpsed) — male, 7, small, short black hair, huge dark eyes, a blue bead on a string at his neck.
**Text:**
- Caption: "Where else would he go? Nobody called him in."
- Shibl (from inside): "Tell the one about her. Tell the one with the hooves."
- Umm Hanan: "Faces to the roof. Hands on your bellies. And don't you dare tell your fathers I told it."
**Mood:** Drowsy, eavesdropping, hot, expectant.

## Slide 2 — The Tale of the Noon Mare
**Scene:** Illustration of the story as Umm Hanan tells it: an empty white lane at noon, every door shut, and down it walks a tall figure half lost in the heat shimmer, upper body a woman in a faded dress with a dark head-cloth hiding hair and face, lower body grey donkey legs bending backwards, small neat hooves on hot stones. Stylised, story-within-story framing with a dusty sepia-white border. Low angle from a child's height.
**Characters:** The Noon Mare — a creature in white noon heat-haze: from the waist up a pale figure in a long, faded, modest dress, with hair and face hidden under a dark head-cloth; from the waist down a grey donkey's legs and hooves; never shown clearly, always half-hidden in the shimmer.
**Text:**
- Caption (Umm Hanan): "There was, and there was not, a creature that walked at noon."
- SFX: *tik… tik… tik*
- Caption (Umm Hanan): "The first rule. She speaks in your mother's voice. Not like your mother. *In* it."
**Mood:** Fabled, uncanny, sun-bleached, stalking.

## Slide 3 — The Second Rule

He goes still as a hare under a falcon's shadow; his left fist clenches on his knee and a shudder runs through him into the dog's chin. Extreme close-up of his face in hard noon light, the lane blurred behind.
**Characters:** Theyab — male, 15, Emirati Bedouin boy, thin, brown sun-darkened skin, thick black eyebrows, short black hair, light amber-gold eyes, ragged sand-coloured kandura, faint glowing golden feather mark on his chest, keeps his left fist closed. **Text:**

- Caption (Umm Hanan): "Your name's been said by someone who loves you, and that's a wall."

## Slide 4 — Hanan
**Scene:** Memory illustration, forty years ago: a tired young mother runs out of her door into an empty white lane at noon; in the dust, small hoofprints two by two lead toward the shaking air, and one small sandal lies on its side. She holds the sandal to her chest. Then back in the arish, the old woman says the daughter's name. Wide shot of the lane with the mother small, sandal in hand.
**Characters:** Young mother (Umm Hanan forty years ago) — young woman, black abaya and black shayla covering all her hair, face uncovered and stricken, brown hands. Ghareeb (in the present, outside the frond wall) — old Arabian saluki hound, very slim and long-legged, deep narrow chest, long narrow head, long silky feathered ears, feathered curved tail, sand-coloured coat, left eye glowing silver, right eye amber, hackles rising.
**Text:**
- Caption (Umm Hanan): "It was her own voice. Her own exact voice. With the tiredness in it."
- Caption (Umm Hanan): "One small sandal. It was warm. Warm as bread just off the plate."
- Girl: "What was the daughter's name?" — Umm Hanan: "Hanan."
- Umm Hanan: "It's a tale. Go to sleep." (Caption: "My hackles rose.")
**Mood:** Devastating, empty, haunted, guilty.
**Image prompt:** Semi-realistic anime seinen manga comic panel, cinematic, full colour, 1920s Emirati Bedouin desert, horror mood, everyone fully and modestly clothed, wide shot of an empty village lane at blinding noon between white mud-brick walls; a young Arab mother in a black abaya and a black shayla fully covering her hair, her face stricken with horror, stands alone clutching one small child's sandal to her chest; in the dust before her a trail of small round hoofprints going two by two leads away toward the end of the lane where the air shakes in heavy heat shimmer and nothing can be seen; the shade by the wall is empty; faded, memory-like colour grading with bleached edges.

## Slide 5 — The Sea Has Its Own
**Scene:** Memory illustration of the pearling banks at night: a dhow at anchor on black water, the crew asleep on deck. A man and two sons walk to the rail and step over, one after another, drawn by a sweet voice from the deep. Back in the arish, Umm Hanan's masked face turned away. Wide night shot, moonlit sea.
**Characters:** Umm Hanan's husband — Arab man, short hair, bare head, plain pearl-diver's clothing. Her elder son — young man, short black hair, bare head, diver's clothing. Her younger son — boy of 14, short black hair, bare head, simple kandura. Umm Hanan (inset, present) — old woman, black abaya and black shayla covering all her hair, gold burqa face mask, kind tired eyes, wrinkled brown hands.
**Text:**
- Shibl: "Was it her who took your men too?"
- Umm Hanan: "No. The sea's not hers. The sea has its own."
- Caption (Umm Hanan): "He said it was my voice. Calling them in, the way I used to call them in from the sun."
- Caption (Umm Hanan): "It's in no book. Go to sleep."
**Mood:** Drowned, hypnotic, grieving, black.

## Slide 6 — "Come In, Theyab"
**Scene:** The children sleep. Shibl comes round the end of the frond wall barefoot into the burning lane, squinting, jug ears pink in the sun, and seriously calls the foundling in by his secret name. Theyab stares, stunned. Two-shot at child height, the empty white lane stretching behind them.
**Characters:** Shibl — male, 7, small, short black hair, huge dark eyes, a blue bead on a string at his neck, barefoot. Theyab — male, 15, Emirati Bedouin boy, thin, brown sun-darkened skin, thick black eyebrows, short black hair, light amber-gold eyes, ragged sand-coloured kandura, faint glowing golden feather mark on his chest, left fist closed. Ghareeb — old Arabian saluki hound, very slim and long-legged, deep narrow chest, long narrow head, long silky feathered ears, feathered curved tail, sand-coloured coat, left eye glowing silver, right eye amber.
**Text:**
- Shibl: "Who calls you in?" — Theyab: "Nobody."
- Shibl: "Come in, Theyab. Come in from the sun."

**Mood:** Tender, startling, fragile, ominous.

## Slide 7 — "Coming, Mama!"

## Slide 8 — "Come In, Laqeet"
**Scene:** Theyab sweeps Shibl into his arms and turns — and the voice changes to every mother's voice, calling him for the first time in his life. His back goes loose; he takes a step toward the shimmer. Then Shibl wakes and says his name, loud and cross. Theyab turns his back on the voice and walks up the lane, the dog herding him, hoofsteps following exactly as fast. Medium shot from the front, the creature a blur over his shoulder.

## Slide 9 — "Mama Called Me"

Saqi stands over Theyab; after the boy whispers "Mama called me," Saqi's face drains white and he strikes Theyab, who drops to one knee.
