# Chapter 4: Never Tell the Wind Who You Are

## Slide 1 — The Sheikh's Barred Door
**Scene:** Before dawn. Theyab, sleepless, Shibl's cold sandal in hand, beats on the door of the only house with a wind-tower. Sheikh Mazyad opens it a hand's width by lamplight and dismisses the grey as sea fog, but his eyes slide fearfully east. Then the door shuts and the bar drops. Medium shot from the step, lamplight cutting the dark.
**Characters:** Theyab — male, 15, Emirati Bedouin boy, thin, brown sun-darkened skin, thick black eyebrows, short black hair, light amber-gold eyes, ragged sand-coloured kandura, a faint glowing golden feather mark on his chest, keeps his left fist closed. Sheikh Mazyad — heavy Arab man, short hair, bare head, thick thornbush beard hennaed orange at the tips, fine robe, holding a lamp. Ghareeb — an old Arabian saluki hound (a real dog breed, not a fox): very slim and long-legged, deep narrow chest, long narrow head, long silky feathered ears, feathered curved tail, sand-coloured coat, left eye glowing silver, right eye amber, his shadow a little too long.
**Text:**
- Mazyad: "Sea fog. Off the salt."
- Theyab: "It isn't moving like a storm. It's moving like something that's listening."
- Caption: "Not a lie. Fear. Fear is the lie's older brother."
- Mazyad: "Go back to your tent, Laqeet."
**Mood:** Dismissive, fearful, cold, shut-out.

## Slide 3 — The Four Things

At the flap, leaving, the blind man turns his milky eyes exactly onto the dog. Close-up on the hand, then on Bakhit's face.
**Characters:** Old Bakhit — male, ancient, blind with milky white eyes, short white hair, long white beard, plain robe; a tent full of hanging herbs and small brass bells. ## Slide 4 — Haddaj the Digger
**Scene:** After noon, a stranger on an old grey split-eared she-camel rides into the square from the deep sands. He buys dates from Ghanim, who weighs honestly for once, and they whisper about the foundling's left hand. Then the stranger turns and looks straight across the square into the dog's silver eye. Wide shot of the square, then close-up of his stare.
**Characters:** Haddaj the Digger — male, old and gaunt, hawk nose, short grey hair, bare head, dark bisht, clay under his fingernails, a battered book of buried places wrapped in oiled cloth under his arm, a short shovel on his saddle, an old Martini rifle. Ghanim — male, about 50, fat, short hair, bare head, orange henna-dyed beard, gold rings, crooked brass scales. Ghareeb — old Arabian saluki hound, very slim and long-legged, deep narrow chest, long narrow head, long silky feathered ears, feathered curved tail, sand-coloured coat, left eye glowing silver, right eye amber, lying by Ghanim's back wall.

- Haddaj: "You'll be paid. When it's done." (Caption: "A lie worn smooth.")
- Haddaj: "That's an old dog."

## Slide 5 — Clay on His Eyes
