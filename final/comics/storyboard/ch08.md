- Saqi (Worn) — Male, about 45, heavy, short grey hair, grey beard, rough hands, well-keeper's rope over his shoulder; eyes rolled completely white, black smoke at the mouth, twisted unnatural pose.
- Shibl — Male, 7, small, short black hair, huge dark eyes, a blue bead on a string at his neck.

## Slide 7 — The Fall Into the Eyes

Final beat back in reality: Saqi's mouth speaking in Theyab's own voice, Theyab's fingers starting to open on Shibl's wrist.
