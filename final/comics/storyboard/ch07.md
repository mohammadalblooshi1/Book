# Chapter 7: Never Lower a Bucket After Dark

## Slide 1 — The Rope Goes Tight
**Scene:** Midnight at the well, continuing the moment the well hums back. Low angle from the stone lip: the rope over the wooden roller is suddenly strained taut, Saqi's arms jerked straight, his heels sliding on the stone. He does not let go; he hauls hand over hand. A shimmer of heat with no fire rises from the shaft. Ghareeb is on his feet behind the wall, growling.
**Characters:**
- Saqi — Male, about 45, heavy, short grey hair, grey beard, rough hands, well-keeper's rope over his shoulder (the rope now in his hands, straining).
- Ghareeb — An old Arabian saluki hound (a real dog breed, not a fox): very slim and long-legged, deep narrow chest, long narrow head, long silky feathered ears, feathered curved tail, sand-coloured coat. Left eye glowing silver, right eye amber. His shadow is a little too long.
**Text:**
- Caption (Ghareeb): Every well has someone living in it, little one. That night, the cool smell was gone.
- Caption (Ghareeb): Smokeless fire. Heat with nothing burning.
- SFX: GRRROAN (the roller)
**Mood:** Strained, ominous, rising heat.

## Slide 4 — Free Water
**Scene:** Next morning, bright sun at the well. Saqi fills all four skins of a tall thin Bedouin camel-man, waters his three tired camels, and presses dates and a small coffee cup into his hands, smiling. Ghareeb's hackles stand up along his whole spine. The camel-man looks from the dog to the smile, sets the cup carefully on the well lip, and leads his camels away at a near trot.
**Characters:**
- Saqi — as Slide 1, smiling broadly.
- Ghareeb — as Slide 1, hackles raised.
- The camel-man — tall thin Bedouin, short black hair, bare head, sun-cracked lip, dusty brown kandura, three tired camels.
**Text:**
- Saqi: The well belongs to whoever is thirsty. Go with the Maker's care.
- Caption (Ghareeb): Saqi charged for water the way the sun charges for shade.
**Mood:** Sunny, false, suspicious.

Second beat: Ghareeb thrown back onto the court, every hair standing; Saqi leaning relaxed in his doorway, arms folded, head tilted a little too far.
**Characters:**
- Ghareeb — as Slide 1, silver eye glowing hard.
- Saqi — as Slide 1, arms folded, smiling, head tilted too far.

**Text:**

- Caption (Ghareeb): What stands in the lanes is the robe. What walks in the rows is the rest.

**Mood:** Vertiginous, infernal, exposed.
