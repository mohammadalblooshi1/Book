# Creature Names: Horror Names Only (author's rule)

**Rule:** in the final book (*Never Say Your Name to the Wind*), **never** use the words "jinn", "jinni" or "djinn", or any of the old folklore or classical names (Bu Darya, Umm al-Duwais, Hmarat al-Qayla, Abu Fanous, Umm al-Sibyan, ghoul, ifrit, marid, shaytan, si'lat, shiqq, nasnas, hatif, qareen, jathoom, malboos, zahwi, zawba'a, and so on). Use the **new horror names** below everywhere: narration, dialogue, chapter titles and epigraphs. The *idea* of each creature stays (it comes from real folklore and the old books), but its *name* is ours. This rule applies to the final book only; the research files keep the real names.

**Unchanged**, since these are human things rather than creatures: Mutawwa' Hilal, hirz (amulet), iron, blue bead, falaj, ghaf, and all people and place names.

Ghareeb can mention once, late in the book, that "the old people had other names for them, names I won't put in your ears, little one", to show the names are deliberately withheld. That fits *Never say your name…*: even the creatures' names are dangerous to speak.
