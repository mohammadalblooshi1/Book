# The Ember of the Destiny Desert: 120 Questions to Make It Unique

Answer these in any order, short or long. Skip any you're unsure of, and I'll suggest options for those.
You can write your answers under each question here, or reply in chat by number (e.g. "7: he has a scar on his hand").

---

## A. Zayn: The Boy (1–20)
1. What is the very first memory Zayn has?
2. What does he do every day in Harrow's End? Does he have a job (water carrier, goat herder, scavenger)?
3. Where does he sleep: alone in a ruin, in a stable, on Hesset's floor?
4. What is one small habit he has (counting stars, drawing in the sand, whistling)?
5. What is his favourite thing in the world before the journey begins?
6. What food does he love, and what does it taste like to him?
7. Does he have any scar or injury besides the feather mark? How did he get it?
8. What is one thing that makes him laugh?
9. What makes him angry fastest?
10. What is his secret dream that he has never told anyone?
11. How does it feel physically when his light comes out: warm, painful, like burning, like crying?
12. What colour does his light turn when he is angry or afraid?
13. What does he say to himself when he feels most alone?
14. Does he pray to the Weaver? If so, what does he pray for?
15. What is one wrong decision he makes that causes real harm?
16. What does he carry in his bag on the journey?
17. Is he good at fighting, or does he learn on the way? Who teaches him?
18. What weapon, if any, does he use?
19. What does he finally believe about himself on the last night of his life?
20. What are his last spoken words?

## B. Kiri: The Narrator Fox (21–32)
21. How does Kiri first appear to Zayn: does he save him, steal from him, or follow him?
22. Can everyone hear Kiri talk, or only the bearer?
23. What is Kiri's personality flaw (proud, sarcastic, secretive, afraid of water)?
24. What did Kiri feel for the bearers who failed?
25. Which past bearer does Kiri miss the most, and why?
26. What is one secret Kiri keeps from Zayn until the very end?
27. Does Kiri ever lie, even once, in the whole story? Why?
28. What does Kiri eat, if he eats at all?
29. Does Kiri have any power besides his truth-glowing tail?
30. Does Kiri ever disagree with the Weaver's plan?
31. How does Kiri's narration sound: formal, poetic, funny, sad, or a mix?
32. What is Kiri's very last line in the book?

## C. Prince Soren (33–46)
33. Why exactly was Soren accused of treason? What did people think he did?
34. What is Soren's greatest skill (sword, archery, riding, speaking, strategy)?
35. How did Soren survive alone in the desert after his exile?
36. What is the first thing Soren says to Zayn?
37. What do Zayn and Soren argue about?
38. What is one funny moment between them?
39. What does Soren teach Zayn, and what does Zayn teach Soren?
40. Does Soren have brothers, friends, or someone he loves back in Taharyn?
41. What is the story behind Soren's broken sword?
42. Did Soren know he was going to die? Did he leave Zayn a message?
43. Who finds Soren's body: Zayn, Kiri, or someone else?
44. How long are they friends before he dies: days, weeks, months?
45. What is the one promise Zayn makes to Soren?
46. Does Soren appear in any vision after his death?

## D. Old Hesset (47–54)
47. How old is Hesset really?
48. What did he see before he went blind?
49. What healing tools does he use (herbs, bells, songs, sand)?
50. What is the hardest question he asks Zayn?
51. Does Hesset ever betray Zayn, even by keeping silent?
52. What happened between Hesset and Malek centuries ago?
53. Does Hesset leave the village and join the journey at any point?
54. What does Hesset give Zayn before he leaves?

## E. Daruun, the Father (55–62)
55. What is Daruun's job or life now (hermit, mercenary, guard, nomad)?
56. How many times does he save Zayn before revealing who he is?
57. How does Zayn react when he learns the Veiled Rider is his father: rage, silence, tears?
58. What does Daruun say to explain why he abandoned his son?
59. Does Zayn forgive him quickly, slowly, or only at the very end?
60. Does Daruun try to stop Zayn's sacrifice?
61. Why did Daruun hide under another name, and where did he live?
62. What does Daruun do with his life after Zayn's death?

## F. Tamir / Brother Seven, the Older Brother (63–68)
63. What weapon or fighting style does Brother Seven use?
64. How many times does he fight Zayn before the truth comes out?
65. Does Kiri recognise him before Zayn does?
66. What are the words of the song he hums?
67. When his mask first cracks, what exactly does he see?
68. Does he ever choose to help Zayn while still in the cult?

## G. Vizier Orrin & the Unnamed (69–80)
69. What does Orrin look like up close? Any detail that feels "wrong"?
70. What was the name Orrin gave away to the Hollow?
71. How does Orrin trick Zayn into trusting him? What does he offer?
72. What is the moment Zayn realises Orrin betrayed him?
73. What happens to Zayn while he is the cult's prisoner?
74. Where is the Unnamed's hidden temple or base?
75. What ritual do new members go through to give up their names?
76. Are there any children in the cult? How does Zayn feel about that?
77. Who is the cult's strongest fighter besides Brother Seven?
78. Does any cult member secretly want to escape?
79. What happens to Orrin in the very end: does he live, die, or wander?
80. Is there a second traitor besides Orrin?

## H. Malek, Keeper of the Hollow (81–86)
81. How does Malek speak to Zayn: in dreams, through Husks, face to face?
82. What is the most convincing argument Malek makes?
83. Is there a moment the reader feels sorry for Malek?
84. What did Malek look like when he was human?
85. Did Malek have a family or a best friend before he fell?
86. What are Malek's last words when he is healed?

## I. The World & Magic (87–100)
87. What do people in this world call the sun, moon and stars?
88. What do people use for money?
89. What animals besides camels and foxes live in the desert?
90. What is a common greeting or blessing people say?
91. What is a common curse or insult?
92. What festivals or holidays do people celebrate?
93. How do people bury their dead?
94. Is there writing and reading, or are stories passed by voice only?
95. Are there other kingdoms or cities beyond Taharyn and Harrow's End?
96. What lies beyond the Destiny Desert? Ocean, mountains, nothing?
97. What are the eleven earlier bearers' names, or at least a few of them?
98. Can the Light be stolen? What happens if someone tries?
99. Does the Light have any weakness besides the sacrifice?
100. What is one rule of the desert everyone knows ("never walk at night," "never say your name to the wind")?

## J. Action & Big Scenes (101–110)
101. What is the first fight scene in the book?
102. What is the most frightening creature encounter?
103. Which scene should make readers cry the most?
104. Which scene should make readers gasp in shock?
105. Is there a chase scene? Where: sandstorm, city rooftops, canyon?
106. Does Zayn ever lose control of his power and hurt someone innocent?
107. How does Zayn escape from the cult's prison?
108. What happens in the great battle of Taharyn?
109. Does King Absalon survive the battle?
110. Who is the "king's last heir" held hostage at the end: Soren's sibling?

## K. Meaning, Style & the Book Itself (111–120)
111. What is the single message you want teen readers to remember?
112. Is there a quote or saying you personally love that should appear in the book?
113. Which books or films do you want this to feel like (but still be original)?
114. Should each chapter start with a quote, a legend, or Kiri speaking?
115. Do you want chapter titles? What style (poetic, one word, numbers only)?
116. Do you want a map at the front of the book?
117. Do you want illustrations? Of what?
118. Should there be a sequel hook about the newborn bearer?
119. What is the newborn bearer's name?
120. Should the final book title stay *The Ember of the Destiny Desert*, or do you want a new one?
