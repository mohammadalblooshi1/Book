# Continuity Sheet: Fixed Facts for All Chapters

Read with `world-bible.md` and `outline.md`. If they disagree, **this sheet wins** for the details below.

## Style rules (every chapter)
- First-person narration by **Kiri** (a male fox), in a mix of sarcasm and sadness. He speaks to an unseen listener, sometimes addressing them ("you"), occasionally as "little one" near the end. He hints at the future ("I didn't know then…"). Match the voice of Chapters 1–2 exactly.
- YA (teen readers): dark and intense, with lots of action. Real danger, but no gore or graphic torture.
- File format, the same as Chapters 1–2:
  - `# Chapter N`
  - `## Title`
  - a blockquote with an italic legend line plus an attribution line (`— …`)
  - `---`
  - the prose, with `---` between scenes
  - ending with `*End of Chapter N*`
- About **3,000–3,600 words** per chapter.
- In-world terms: the sun is **the Fire**, the moon is **the Ash** (full Ash, thin Ash), the stars are **the Embers**. Money is **water tokens**. No real-world weekdays, months or brand names. Greeting: "May the sand remember you." Curse: "May the dunes forget you." Rule: "Never say your name to the wind."
- Zayn **cannot lie** (the feather mark burns if he tries). Kiri's **tail glows when someone tells the truth and dims when someone lies**. Use this often.
- Every time Zayn heals with the Light, he sees the person's past and future, **and a flash of his own death** (the full Ash, the Crown Dune, fire). It gets clearer and longer each time.
- When Zayn is afraid or angry, his light goes **dim and grey**. When he acts for others, it goes **gold**. Each use leaves **golden cracks** on his skin, which fade slowly.
- Women are minor characters only: Auntie Sabba (Ch 1, 3, 20), Mother Yusa (Ch 17), Talia (Ch 20). Brief mentions of someone's mother are fine.

## Fixed names (use these exactly)
- **Tobin**: Bram's little brother, age 7. Emptied by the grey at the end of Ch 2 and healed by Zayn in Ch 3.
- **Bram's father**: the well-keeper, **Hadric**.
- **Oszkar**: the headman of Harrow's End. **Garrem**: the trader.
- **Nim**: the palace stable boy who was emptied by the Hollow. Soren hid him in the palace and tried to cure him. Soren's sword broke protecting Nim from the guards. Nim's fate: Orrin had him "taken away" (he is never found).
- **Prince Idan** (10): Soren's brother. **King Absalon**: their father.
- **Vizier Orrin**: his real name is **Ezren**. He has faded grey eyes.
- **Brother One**: Orrin's giant, silent bodyguard.
- **Tamir / Brother Seven**: twin grey blades. He hums a phoenix song.
- **Daruun**: the Veiled Rider, on a pale camel, with a long hunting spear.
- **Old Hesset**: male, blind, 300 years old, keeps truth bells.
- The falcon messengers, sand lizards and giant scorpions are as in the bible.

## Zayn's details to weave in
Scavenger. Patched tent. Back scar from stones. Dates and honey. Touches his mark when nervous. Hates "monster". Dreams of being a hero. "The sand remembers." Doesn't pray. Self-taught fighter. Scavenged knife, then Soren's broken sword.
