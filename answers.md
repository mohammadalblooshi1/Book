# Story Answers

## A. Zayn
1. **First memory:** Lying alone in the sand, crying, with a fox watching him.
2. **Daily life:** Scavenger. He digs in old ruins for metal and things to trade.
3. **Sleeps:** In his own tent.
4. **Habit:** Touches his feather mark when he's nervous.
5. **Favourite thing:** Old stories, especially Hesset's legends of the Phoenix-Born.
6. **Favourite food:** Dates and honey, a rare treat he saves for hard days.
7. **Scar:** On his back, from a stone villagers threw at him when he was a child.
8. **What makes him laugh:** Soren's jokes. The prince is the first person who really makes him laugh.
9. **Angers him fastest:** Being called "monster".
10. **Secret dream:** To be a hero like the Phoenix-Born in the stories.
11. **How the light feels:** Burning in his chest, spreading out from the feather mark.
12. **Light when angry or afraid:** Turns dim and grey, as if the Hollow is touching it.
13. **What he tells himself when alone:** "The sand remembers."
14. **Prayer:** He does not pray to the Weaver.
15. **His worst mistake:** He leaves Soren alone after an argument, and that is the night Soren dies.
16. **In his bag:** A scavenged knife.
17. **Fighting:** Self-taught, from surviving village bullies and the ruins.
18. **Weapon:** Soren's broken sword, which he takes after Soren dies.
19. **His belief on the last night:** "I am the hero." His secret dream comes true.
20. **Last words:** "I'm coming, Soren."

## B. Kiri
21. **First meeting:** Kiri was the fox watching baby Zayn in the sand, which is Zayn's first memory.
22. **Who hears him:** Everyone can hear Kiri talk, and he is often rude.
23. **Flaw:** Sarcastic. He hides his love behind rude jokes.
24. **Feelings for the failed bearers:** Anger, especially at Malek.
25. **Past bearer he misses most:** Malek, his first bearer and the one who broke his heart.
26. **Secret until the end:** Kiri saw Orrin kill Soren but had no proof.
27. **Does he lie:** Once, to protect Zayn. His tail goes dark, and Zayn sees it and is shocked.
28. **Food:** Scorpions, which he crunches loudly to disgust Zayn.
29. **Other power:** Kiri is **half monster**. He has the strength to lift and hold very heavy things, far more than a fox should.
30. **Disagrees with the Weaver:** Yes, angrily. He shouts at the sky: "Not this one. Not again."
31. **Narration voice:** A mix of sarcasm and sadness. Sharp, funny comments alongside deep grief.
32. **Last line of the book:** "Wake up, little one." (said to the newborn bearer)
- **Kiri's other half (follow-up):** Kiri is **half Hollow creature**. Part of him came from the darkness itself, which is a secret danger.

## C. Prince Soren
33. **Why accused of treason:** He secretly hid and protected a Hollow victim inside the palace.
34. **Greatest skill:** Sword fighting. He is the best blade in Kharesh, even with a broken sword.
35. **Survival in exile:** He hunts shadow creatures and sells their ash.
36. **First words to Zayn:** "You glow. That's weird."
37. **What they argue about:** The sacrifice. Soren learns Zayn must die and refuses to accept it.
38. **Funny moment:** Soren tries Kiri's scorpions and gets sick.
39. **What they teach each other:** Soren teaches Zayn to laugh, and Zayn teaches Soren desert survival.
40. **Who he loves in Kharesh:** His little brother, **Prince Idan** (age 10), who is later the hostage at the end.
41. **The broken sword:** It broke while he was protecting the Hollow victim he hid.
42. **Did he know he'd die:** No idea. It was a total surprise.
43. **Who finds his body:** Desert travellers, who spread the story of "the Prince-Killer".
44. **How long they were friends:** Exactly one full moon cycle. The moon ties to the death vision.
45. **Zayn's promise to Soren:** Never give up, no matter what.
46. **Soren after death:** He appears several times in visions, guiding Zayn like a ghost.

## D. Old Hesset
47. **Real age:** About 300 years. He was the bearer three centuries ago.
48. **Last thing he saw before going blind:** The Crown Dune, the place he ran from.
49. **Healing tools:** Herbs and truth bells.
50. **Hardest question he asks Zayn:** "Would you die for them?" (meaning the people who called him monster)
51. **Does he betray Zayn:** Yes, by silence. He knew Zayn would die and never told him.
52. **Hesset and Malek:** Malek only pretended to be Hesset's friend, the same way Orrin later does with Zayn. Kiri was Malek's only true friend.
53. **Does he join the journey:** Only at the end. He breaks his bond to walk to the Crown Dune.
54. **Gift to Zayn:** A small truth bell, so Zayn can tell who is lying.

## E. Daruun (the father)
55. **His life now:** A creature hunter who hunts Hollow beasts to make up for his failure.
56. **Rescues before the reveal:** Three, each one more mysterious.
57. **Zayn's reaction to the truth:** Tears. He breaks down.
58. **Daruun's explanation:** "To protect you." The cult was hunting them both.
59. **Forgiveness:** Zayn forgives him slowly, over many chapters.
60. **Does he try to stop the sacrifice:** Yes. He begs, then lets go. It is the hardest choice of his life.
61. **Where he hid:** In mountain caves where the dunes couldn't reach him.
62. **After Zayn's death:** He helps Bram and Tamir raise the newborn bearer.

## F. Tamir / Brother Seven
63. **Weapon:** Twin grey blades that cut memories, fast and deadly.
64. **Fights with Zayn before the truth:** Many. He hunts Zayn through the whole book.
65. **Does Kiri know:** Yes, and he keeps it secret.
66. **The song he hums:** A phoenix song about fire and rising wings.
67. **What he sees when his mask cracks:** Himself at five years old, holding his newborn brother.
68. **Does he help Zayn while in the cult:** Secretly, many times. He keeps "missing" Zayn on purpose.

## G. Vizier Orrin & the Unnamed
69. **What looks wrong about Orrin:** Faded grey eyes, like a Husk's.
70. **The name he gave away:** **Ezren**, meaning "helper" in the old tongue, which is ironic.
71. **How he wins Zayn's trust:** He shows Zayn Soren's signet ring and claims Soren gave it to him.
72. **The moment Zayn realises:** Hesset's truth bell rings when Orrin speaks, but too late.
73. **Zayn as a prisoner:** All of it. They try to take his name, drain his light, and make him relive his worst pain. It is the darkest chapter of the book.
74. **The cult's base:** An ancient ship buried in the dunes.
75. **The joining ritual:** New members write their name on cloth and burn it.
76. **Children in the cult:** Yes. The cult steals children, as it did with Tamir, and Zayn wants to save them.
77. **Strongest fighter after Tamir:** **Brother One**, Orrin's giant bodyguard, who never speaks.
78. **Who wants to escape:** Only Tamir.
79. **Orrin's end:** He dies.
80. **Second traitor:** **Bram**. He tells the cult where Zayn is, then regrets it forever.

## H. Malek
81. **How he speaks to Zayn:** First in dreams, then through Husks speaking his words in unison, and finally face to face on the Crown Dune. He gets closer as the story goes on.
82. **His most convincing argument:** "You'll be forgotten. No one remembers the bearers."
83. **When readers pity him:** Only at the very end, when he is healed.
84. **As a human:** A small, ordinary, poor shepherd boy.
85. **Friends or family:** Kiri was his only friend, which is why Kiri misses him most.
86. **Last words when healed:** "I remember my name."

## I. World & Magic
87. **Names for the sky:** The sun is **the Fire**, the moon is **the Ash**, and the stars are **the Embers**.
88. **Money:** Water tokens. Each clay token is worth one cup of water.
89. **Other desert animals:** Falcons (used to carry messages), giant scorpions, sand lizards (riding beasts faster than camels) and dune wolves.
90. **Common blessing:** "May the sand remember you."
91. **Worst curse:** "May the dunes forget you."
92. **Festival:** The **Night of Embers**, when everyone lights a lantern for the lost bearers.
93. **Burial:** The dead are given to the dunes, left on the sand for the desert to take.
94. **Reading and writing:** Only the rich can read. Zayn can't, and Soren teaches him.
95. **Other places:** Many small free oasis towns scattered across the desert, with no other big kingdoms.
96. **Beyond the desert:** A mystery. No one has ever returned from the edge.
97. **The eleven earlier bearers:** Known by titles only, e.g. "The Shepherd" (Malek), "The Blind One" (Hesset), "The Runner" (Daruun).
98. **Can the Light be stolen:** Yes, but stolen light turns grey and destroys the thief.
99. **The Light's weakness:** The cult's grey blades can cut it.
100. **Rule of the desert:** "Never say your name to the wind," because the Hollow might hear it.

## J. Action & Big Scenes
101. **First fight:** Husks attack Harrow's End, and Zayn's light awakens.
102. **Most frightening encounter:** The Veilbeast swallowing a whole caravan in a storm.
103. **Saddest scene:** The sacrifice, as Zayn burns saying "I'm coming, Soren."
104. **Biggest shock:** Kiri is revealed to be half Hollow.
105. **Big chase:** Riding sand lizards through a sandstorm.
106. **Losing control:** Yes. Zayn's light burns Kiri's Hollow half.
107. **Escape from the buried ship:** Kiri uses his monster strength to lift and break open the wreck.
108. **Great battle of Kharesh:** Zayn duels Brother One, Orrin's giant bodyguard.
109. **King Absalon:** He dies in the battle.
110. **Hostage at the end:** Prince Idan, Soren's 10-year-old brother.

## K. Meaning, Style & the Book
111. **Main message for readers:** Your pain has a purpose.
112. **Key quote:** "The end is the beginning."
113. **Should feel like:** *Dune*, *The Alchemist* and *Harry Potter* (but fully original).
114. **Chapter openings:** Each chapter starts with a line from a desert legend.
115. **Chapter titles:** Poetic (e.g. "The Boy the Sand Forgot").
116. **Map:** Yes, a map of the Destiny Desert at the front.
117. **Illustrations:** Yes, for every chapter. Three to four illustrations of key scenes per chapter, in a **3D realistic anime style** (similar in feel to Naruto-era shonen anime, but realistic, not cartoon).
118. **Sequel hook:** Yes. Book 2 follows the newborn bearer.
119. **Newborn bearer's name:** **Aeron**. Bram names him after Zayn's true name.
120. **Final book title:** ***The End Is the Beginning***
- **Bonus (personal details):** None. The story stays fully fictional.
