# The End Is the Beginning: Illustration Prompts

Use these with an AI image generator, or give them to an artist.
Each chapter has **3–4 key scenes**.

---

## How to use
1. Paste the **Style Block** at the start of every prompt, so all the art matches.
2. Add the **Character Sheet** description for any character who appears in the scene.
3. Then add the scene prompt.

### Style Block (paste first)
> *Cinematic 3D realistic anime style, semi-realistic proportions, detailed skin and fabric textures, dramatic volumetric lighting, warm desert golds against cold greys, painterly depth of field, high detail, epic fantasy, not cartoon, not chibi, no text.*

**Optional ending for every prompt:** *"wide cinematic shot, 16:9"* for scenes, or *"portrait, 2:3"* for character moments.

## PART ONE: THE NAMELESS

### Chapter 1: The Boy the Sand Forgot
1. **The death vision:** Zayn standing on the highest dune under a huge full white moon, arms open, golden fire bursting from the feather mark on his chest and spreading into enormous phoenix wings across the night sky.
2. **The ruins:** Zayn crouched inside a half-buried ancient ruin, a knife between his teeth, prying copper from a wall, thin blades of sunlight falling through cracks, dust drifting, Kiri watching from the doorway.
3. **Hesset's tent:** Inside a lamp-lit tent hung with herbs and bronze bells, old blind Hesset telling a story with his hands raised, Zayn sitting cross-legged with shining eyes.
4. **The grey on the horizon:** Zayn alone on top of a dune at night, hand pressed to his glowing chest, looking at a low line of grey mist on the far horizon swallowing the stars, Kiri sitting at the foot of the dune behind him.

### Chapter 2: When the Grey Came Creeping
1. Grey mist rolling into a desert village at dusk, colour draining from clay houses, Husks walking silently through the streets.
2. Zayn cornered against a wall, golden light exploding from his open hands for the first time, Husks recoiling, his face shocked.
3. Kiri sitting on a rooftop speaking, villagers staring at him in terror, his tail glowing bright.

### Chapter 3: Monster
1. Zayn kneeling and healing a small grey-skinned child, golden light flowing from his hands, ghostly memories swirling around them.
2. Villagers throwing stones at Zayn as he runs from the village, Bram shouting in the front, dust and anger.
3. An old woman (Auntie Sabba, grey headscarf, kind tired face) secretly pressing bread into Zayn's hands in a dark alley.
4. The Veiled Rider on his pale camel, spear raised, driving back shadow wolves with ember eyes under starlight.

### Chapter 4: The Bell and the Feather
1. Old Hesset placing a broken golden feather and a small bronze bell into Zayn's hands by lamplight.
2. Zayn and Kiri walking into the endless dunes at dawn, long shadows, the village small behind them.
3. Dunes shifting and flowing like waves at night under the stars, Zayn watching in awe.

## PART TWO: THE DESERT THAT WRITES

### Chapter 5: Where the Dunes Speak
1. Giant glowing symbols forming in the sand of the dunes at night, Zayn staring, unable to read them.
2. A serpent made of black sand bursting up from a dune to strike at Zayn.
3. Brother Seven standing on a dune ridge in moonlight, twin grey blades drawn, white mask, cloak whipping in wind.

### Chapter 6: The Valley of Echoes
1. Zayn alone in a deep narrow red canyon, his shouted words visible as ghostly echoing shapes of his fears.
2. A dream: a huge shadow figure with a blurred face leaning over a sleeping Zayn.
3. Kiri on a rock, fur bristling, glaring furiously up at the sky.

### Chapter 7: The Caravan and the Beast
1. A trading caravan of camels and sand lizards under a sky turning black at noon, falcons fleeing.
2. A colossal beast of storm and shadow rising from a sandstorm and swallowing the caravan, tiny figures fleeing.
3. The Veiled Rider's hand pulling Zayn out of deep sand as the storm roars.

### Chapter 8: The Singing Wells
1. An oasis of glowing humming water at dusk, Soren fighting a pack of shadow wolves alone with a broken sword.
2. Zayn and Soren back to back, golden light and steel, shadow wolves circling.
3. Zayn kneeling at the water, seeing a memory reflected: a small boy's hand holding a baby's hand.

## PART THREE: ONE TURN OF THE ASH

### Chapter 9: A Prince with a Broken Sword
1. Soren telling his story by a campfire, the broken sword across his knees, Zayn listening, stars above.
2. Soren green-faced and sick after eating a scorpion, Kiri smug, Zayn laughing for the first time.
3. Soren drawing letters in the sand with a stick, teaching Zayn to read.

### Chapter 10: The City of Glass
1. Ruins of a city made of melted glass glowing orange at sunset in the middle of the desert.
2. Giant ancient murals on glass walls showing eleven figures with feather marks, each facing a dune under a moon.
3. Close-up of Kiri's glowing tail going dark as he speaks, Zayn's face realising he is being lied to.

### Chapter 11: Riders of the Storm
1. Zayn and Soren riding giant sand lizards at full speed through a raging sandstorm, masked cultists and shadow wolves chasing.
2. Kiri transforming into a giant shadow-fox monster, lifting a huge fallen stone pillar.
3. Zayn's golden light flaring out of control and burning the shadow-beast Kiri, who howls in pain.

### Chapter 12: The Night of the Full Ash
1. Zayn and Soren arguing face to face under a giant full moon on a dune.
2. Zayn walking away alone into the dark, Soren a small figure behind him.
3. Zayn kneeling in the sand at dawn beside a broken sword, desert travellers in the distance.

## PART FOUR: THE UNNAMED

### Chapter 13: Nameless Again
1. Zayn sitting alone in a vast empty desert, tiny in the frame, grey sky.
2. Soren's ghost appearing in soft golden light, hand on Zayn's shoulder.
3. Vizier Orrin with kind smile and grey eyes, holding out a royal signet ring.

### Chapter 14: The Ringing of the Bell
1. Orrin and Zayn sharing a warm meal in a small oasis-town room, lamplight.
2. Bram in ragged clothes whispering to a masked cultist in a dark alley.
3. A small bronze bell ringing on Zayn's belt as white faceless masks close in from the shadows.

### Chapter 15: The Buried Ship
1. An enormous ancient wooden ship half-buried in dunes under moonlight, lantern glow inside its broken hull.
2. A ritual: masked men burning cloths with names written on them, firelight on white masks.
3. Brother Seven's white mask cracking down the middle, golden light shining through, a memory of a boy holding a baby.
4. Kiri as a huge shadow-beast lifting the entire buried ship, sand pouring off it, children escaping.

### Chapter 16: The Rider Unveiled
1. The Veiled Rider pulling down his grey face wrap to reveal a weathered, tearful face.
2. Zayn crying in his father's arms.
3. Firelit mountain caves high above the desert.
4. Zayn opening the hollow hilt of the broken sword and reading a rolled message.

## PART FIVE: THE END IS THE BEGINNING

### Chapter 17: The Walls of Vel'Mourne
1. A great red-stone walled city lined with thousands of burning lanterns, grey mist pressing against the walls.
2. A large cheerful palace cook (Mother Yusa, apron, flour on her hands) hiding Zayn in a storeroom full of sacks and honey jars.
3. Zayn standing before a king on a red-stone throne, Orrin smiling beside him.

### Chapter 18: The Night of Embers
1. City streets full of floating festival lanterns as Husks and masked cultists flood in.
2. Zayn with a broken sword duelling a giant silent masked warrior on palace stairs.
3. The fallen king in the throne hall, lanterns burning around him.
4. Orrin dragging a young prince toward the desert gate.

### Chapter 19: The Crown Dune
1. Zayn facing a towering shadow figure on the highest dune under a giant full moon.
2. Daruun on his knees, letting go of Zayn's hand.
3. Zayn exploding into golden phoenix fire, enormous wings of light over the desert.
4. Dunes far below forming a glowing golden word in the sand, seen from above.

### Chapter 20: Ash into Gold
1. The entire desert turning gold at dawn, grey mist fading away.
2. Kiri sitting alone on top of the Crown Dune at night, tiny against the stars, guarding golden ash.
3. Village lanterns rising into the night sky on the Night of Embers, an old woman lighting the first one.
4. Kiri curled beside a baby's cradle, the baby's chest glowing with a tiny feather mark.
